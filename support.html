Ways to Give

There are many ways to make an impact. Help us keep our literacy programs completely free for children.

Monetary Donations

Your financial contributions go directly toward printing custom learning curriculum, funding workshop spaces, purchasing books, and managing programmatic overhead.

Donate Funds

Donate Supplies

We eagerly accept free physical items that actively help us teach kids, including elementary school level books, workbooks, and basic stationery supplies (pencils, markers, notebooks).

To coordinate item drop-offs or direct shipping, please reach out to:
contact@readlinkproject.org

Volunteer with Us

Help us build the bridge to literacy! We are looking for passionate, hands-on changemakers to join our team.

Resource Prep

Help us review, sort, and organize book donations and assemble educational workbook packages ready for local neighborhoods.

Outreach Support

Assist in transporting materials and setting up outdoor distribution tables during our community-wide book drives.

Creative Contribution

Use your writing, illustrative, or graphic layout talents to design highly interactive, fun learning tools for students.
